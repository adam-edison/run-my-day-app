# Captured for Later

These behaviors belong in OpenSpec specs once those exist. Each one moves to its spec when that spec is written.

- When a move up waits, Run My Day tells you why, such as "Push-ups is ready for L3, but the Workout block is 10 minutes short". It also lists the short block's entries with their estimates, so you can choose what to shorten, remove, or move.
- A new task gets one step with the task's title and estimate, so a simple task needs only a title and an estimate.
- You can capture a task from any screen, or by voice during a routine, without pausing the routine.
- Run My Day announces each appointment 10 minutes and 5 minutes before it starts, such as "Dentist in 10 minutes". After the appointment, you resume the paused routine yourself.
- Extend offers quick options that you set as a preference, 1, 5, and 10 minutes by default.
- While a run is paused, the phone keeps its sticky notification and the web app shows a notification that the run is waiting to resume. A pause gives no repeating reminder.
- Batch editing applies step settings, such as warnings and nagging, to many steps at once.
- The shipped start sounds come from a free sound library, chosen to be smooth and not annoying.
- Overdue tasks are easy to find quickly, in a view of their own, so they never get lost.
- An achievement view shows its trophy, the conditions it took, and the history records that met them.
- Metric analysis can group entries by hour of day, such as when you drink most of your water or when your self-reported energy is lowest.
- When Run My Day refuses a setting, such as a circular condition, it shows a plain message that explains how to fix it.
- A recurring task shows when it was last completed, in a place you can find at a glance, such as next to its due date.
- A task can be marked optional. This flag is separate from every other task setting. When an optional task is not completed, Run My Day does not count a skip. A recurring optional task moves to its next due date. A non-recurring optional task moves to the next day. An example is "Clean the stovetop if it is dirty".
