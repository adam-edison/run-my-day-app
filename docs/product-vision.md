# Product Vision

Run My Day is an audio-guided assistant that runs your whole day from your calendar.

## Purpose

- Fights distractions and keeps you on track with your calendar.
- Guides each transition from task to task and from appointment to appointment.
- Works at the level of detail you choose, from a few simple prompts to fully scripted days.

## Scope

Run My Day manages your tasks, projects, routines, habits, goals, achievements, and progressions.

A progression is a sequence of levels, such as "run 1 km, then 3 km, then 5 km". Your rules move you up or down a level automatically. The current level sets what the day asks of you.

## Principles

- **You stay in control.** Run My Day is a deterministic system. It follows your rules and never makes decisions on its own.
- **The calendar is the hub.** Your calendar holds your schedule. Run My Day reads it and never changes it.
- **AI works from the outside.** AI agents use Run My Day through an MCP server, with the same powers you have. No AI makes decisions inside the app.

## Audience

Run My Day starts as a single-user, self-hosted app for its author. It is designed from the start for a paid hosted version, so every piece of data belongs to a user account.
